# Bard

An app to explore Shakespeare's vocabulary.

## What it does

You are given 10 random words from *Shakespeare*'s works.
Then you may select one of these words and get the 10 most 
similar words to it, still from *Shakespeare*. You may repeat 
this process as much as you like.

## Instructions

Type: `uv run -m bard` into your shell. 
If you do not have uv, you can download it [here](https://github.com/astral-sh/uv).
